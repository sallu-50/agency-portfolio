Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know Before Starting

How fast can we launch a pilot annotation project?

For standard computer vision and text labeling tasks with clear guidelines, our lead annotators can set up your taxonomy and begin a sample benchmark pilot within 24 to 48 hours.

Can our team provide our own internal labeling tooling?

Yes. We regularly work within clients’ existing environments—including Labelbox, CVAT, Scale Nucleus, Roboflow, Label Studio, or proprietary in-house web portals connected via secure VPN.

How do you maintain a 99.8% precision consensus rate?

We deploy a multi-tier QA hierarchy: Tier 1 primary annotation is audited by Tier 2 QA algorithms (automated overlap & IoU checks), followed by Tier 3 senior domain lead sign-off before batch delivery.
